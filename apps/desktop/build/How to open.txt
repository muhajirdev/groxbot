Groxbot for Mac (Apple Silicon)

1. Drag Groxbot into Applications.
2. This build is not signed. In Finder, right-click Groxbot and choose Open.
   macOS will warn. Click Open. You only do this once.
3. Sign in with the same account as https://app.groxbot.com

Intel Macs are not in this file.
Help: https://groxbot.com/download
